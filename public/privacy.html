Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information BD Situation Intelligence ("we", "us") collects, why, and the choices you have. The service is operated by The Asia Times Company Ltd, which is responsible for your information.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you create an account: your email address, your password (stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider, never in readable form) and, if you give it, your name.
  • Sign-in records. The dates and times you sign in, and technical records our sign-in provider keeps for security, such as IP address and browser type.
  • Technical data. Like any website, our hosting provider receives your IP address, browser and device details and the pages you request, and keeps short-lived logs for security and reliability.
  • Settings on your device. Your dashboard layout, panel choices and similar preferences are stored in your own browser, not on our servers.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Cookies and local storage

  • bdsi_at: a strictly necessary cookie that keeps you signed in. It expires when your session expires or when you log out.
  • Browser local storage holds your sign-in session and your dashboard preferences. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings; you will then need to sign in again.
  • When you play a live TV stream, the video is provided by YouTube, which may set its own cookies under Google's privacy policy.

How we use information

  • To create and secure your account, sign you in and send account emails (confirmation, password reset, sign-in links).
  • To run, protect and improve the service, including preventing abuse and fixing faults.
  • To answer you when you contact us.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not send marketing emails unless you separately agree to receive them.

Service providers

We use trusted providers who process information on our behalf, only for the purposes above:

  • Supabase — accounts and sign-in (data stored in Mumbai, India).
  • Resend — sending account emails.
  • Vercel — website hosting and delivery.
  • Upstash — data cache for dashboard content (no account information).
  • Map tiles, news feeds and video streams are loaded from their providers, such as OpenFreeMap and YouTube, which receive your IP address when your browser requests them.

Some providers may process information outside Bangladesh. Where they do, we rely on their contractual and security commitments to protect it.

How long we keep it

We keep account information for as long as your account is open. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete it within 30 days, except where we must keep limited records by law. Hosting and security logs are kept for a short period, normally no more than 30 days.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop using it, by writing to contact@bdnews.si from the email address on your account. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local data protection law, including the right to complain to a data protection authority. We will respond within 30 days.

Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords and access controls to protect your information. No online service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

The service is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, tell signed-in users by email or on the site.
